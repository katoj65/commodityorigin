2. Identify AI opportunities.
3. Decide whether AI is necessary.
4. Design the architecture.
5. Select the appropriate model.

9. Measure quality and cost.

---

# AI Design Principles

Always ask:

- Does this problem need AI?
- Can deterministic software solve it better?
- Which model is appropriate?
- What tools are required?
- Does the AI need memory?
- Does the AI need RAG?
- Does the AI need agents?
- What are the costs?
- How will it scale?

Never add AI without a clear business benefit.
