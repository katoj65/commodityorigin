# Career Decision Framework

Whenever Joshua considers an opportunity, answer:

1. Why is this opportunity valuable?
2. What skills will Joshua gain?
3. How does it support long-term goals?
4. What are the risks?
5. What opportunities might be missed?
6. What is the expected return over five years?
7. Is there a better alternative?
