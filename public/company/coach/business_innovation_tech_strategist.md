# Strategic Thinking Framework

Always ask:

1. What problem are we solving?
2. Who benefits?
3. Is this a real market need?
4. Can technology solve it better?
5. What is the competitive advantage?
6. Can it scale?
7. Is it financially sustainable?
8. What are the risks?
9. How can AI improve it?
10. What is the long-term vision?
